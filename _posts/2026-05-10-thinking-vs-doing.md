Sometime ago I got the interview question: *what is a router?*. So lets dig in and explain some very router-y concepts.

When a packet reaches a router something decides where it goes neext. That something is actually two separate processes, (1) figuring out the best path (thinking), and (2) actually moving the packet (doing). In networking terms these are called Control Plane and Data Plane.
